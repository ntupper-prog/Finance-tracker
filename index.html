Live Open Banking

To add live bank feeds, the next version would integrate with a regulated Open Banking provider. You would choose your bank, authenticate on the bank's own website or app, and grant read-only access.

For a genuinely private implementation, account tokens should be stored on a secure backend, not inside the app's HTML or local browser storage. We would also need to choose a provider, review costs and supported banks, and add a privacy policy and consent/revocation flow.

Never enter your online banking password into Pocket Ledger. This prototype has no live bank connection.
